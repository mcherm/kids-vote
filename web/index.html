Kids Vote!

Kids vote to decide what event to hold.

How it works

  • Come to Normandy Park on Election Day (Tuesday, November 3) from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM and vote for the event you want to hold.
  • Vote for a Glowstick Dance Party or an Arts and Crafts Party. Any kid may vote.
  • Optional: Bring your parents so they can vote in the grown-up election!
  • At the end of the night we will count the votes and announce the winner.
  • Anyone may come on Sunday, November 15 for the party!
